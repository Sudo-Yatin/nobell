01How to read this

This roadmap is sequenced around de-risking the two things that are unproven, not around building features. If bet 1 or bet 2 from the overview turns out false, most of what comes after Phase 1 is wasted work — so we spend real time confirming them before building anything expensive.

02Week 0 — prove the mic works

Before writing pipeline code, spend two days recording real store audio on an ordinary phone, in a pocket and on a lanyard, and listening to it. This is the cheapest possible test of the single riskiest assumption in the product.

What we do
  • Record 3–4 real associate-customer conversations at a live or friendly store
  • Try phone in shirt pocket vs. on a lanyard at chest height
  • Manually transcribe a sample and compare against what Voicemaker returns
  • Note background noise conditions: quiet showroom vs. busy floor
What tells us to stop or continue
  • Continue as planned if the customer's side is intelligible to a human listener in most recordings.
  • Revisit mic placement (lanyard, not pocket) if pocket audio is bad but lanyard audio is fine.
  • Escalate to external mic / Phase 2 hardware if even lanyard audio can't reliably capture the customer.

Also verify in this window: Android battery-manager kill behaviour on at least one aggressive OEM device (Xiaomi, Oppo, or similar) over a full multi-hour recording session — see mobile app & recording.

03Phase 1 — single-store pilot

Phone mic only, one store, small enough that we can watch it closely and cheap enough to be wrong about parts of it.

In scope
  • React Native app: start/pause/end shift, chunked upload
  • Supabase backend, storage, edge functions
  • Voicemaker transcription with multi-channel diarization
  • Segmentation + rubric scoring pipeline
  • Daily feedback via app and/or WhatsApp
  • Weekly manager dashboard
  • Hindi + English support
Explicitly deferred
  • External mics or dedicated hardware
  • Languages beyond Hindi/English
  • Sales attribution to conversations
  • Live/real-time coaching
Exit criteria

The success metrics table in the PRD — usable-capture rate, coverage, transcription accuracy, score agreement with a human manager, and whether associates say they'd keep using it.

04Phase 2 — scale & hardware

Only entered if Phase 1's exit criteria are met. Two separate expansions, not necessarily simultaneous:

  • More stores, same store type. Tests whether the pipeline holds under real operational variance — different noise floors, different manager habits, different Wi-Fi reliability.
  • External mic / dedicated hardware. Only if Week 0 or Phase 1 shows phone-mic audio quality is the ceiling on everything else. Adds cost and rollout friction, so only pursued if the data says the phone mic genuinely can't get there.

Languages beyond Hindi and English, and sales attribution via POS integration, are Phase 2 candidates but not commitments — see what we are not doing for why each is deferred rather than ruled out.
